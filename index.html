Frequently Asked Questions

Is DueLink custodial?

No. DueLink never holds your funds — everything settles directly between the two parties' wallets through non-custodial smart contracts on Arc.

Do I need a crypto wallet to use DueLink?

No — Circle Developer Wallets let you get started with just an email. No seed phrase, no gas token to buy. Crypto-native users can connect an external wallet instead.

What happens if the other party doesn't pay?

It depends on the Link type. Escrow Trades auto-release or route to arbitration, Loans use posted collateral or affect reputation, and Invoices accrue a late fee and stay open until paid.

Is DueLink regulated?

DueLink is a non-custodial protocol — funds move directly between the two parties' wallets, we never take custody. For large or business-critical obligations, we recommend consulting your own advisor.

Will DueLink work on other chains?

DueLink is purpose-built for Arc's USDC-native gas and sub-second finality. Cross-chain USDC via Unified Balance is on the roadmap, so a Link could pull funds you hold on other chains.
